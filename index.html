What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and compare details such as category, size, color, seller information and estimated costs. Its usefulness depends on how accurately each entry is recorded and how recently it was checked.

The research method

A useful list starts with better questions.

01 / DISCOVER

Choose a purpose first

Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a warm layer, a coordinated set or a small accessory. Browse one category at a time to keep comparisons relevant.

02 / COMPARE

Read beyond the photograph

Check measurements, materials, color options and seller descriptions. Separate confirmed information from details that still need an answer.

03 / ORGANIZE

Keep a decision-ready shortlist

Save the product URL, selected variant, price currency and date checked. Add shipping estimates separately and revisit the listing before ordering.

Straight answers

Before you build your list.

A few practical answers about this guide, product research and the information worth checking.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and compare details such as category, size, color, seller information and estimated costs. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy and freshness of its entries.

Is this an official Litbuy website?

No. This is an independent fashion discovery guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, and it does not provide a Litbuy account, checkout or official spreadsheet.

Does this page include a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides category links and a comparison method. You can use the suggested fields to create your own shortlist in a spreadsheet application.

What should I check before choosing a size?

Compare the listing's measurements with an item you already own. For clothing, check chest width, length and sleeve measurements. For footwear, check the size chart and foot-length guidance where provided.

Does a product photograph prove authenticity or availability?

No. Photographs are visual references. Check the actual seller's description, provenance information, stock, measurements and return conditions before making a purchase.
